Help

Short answers to the things clients ask most. If you still need us, email is fastest.

How do I change my contact information?
Open the Site content tab. You can update your phone number, email, address, business hours and company intro yourself. Save once and the change applies across your whole website.
How do style changes work?
Open the Style tab and choose another design. Your care plan includes one free style change each year; additional changes are $100 each and the price is shown before you confirm. A style change only replaces the design skin — your content, pages and web address stay the same.
How do I connect my old domain?
Use the step-by-step wizard in the Domain tab. It shows the two DNS records to add and gives you a message you can send to your old provider. We never contact your old provider for you, so you stay in control of that account.
How do I cancel the care plan?
In Overview, choose “Cancel auto-renew.” Your website stays live until the end of the paid or included period. After that, it enters a 10-month grace period during which you can log in and renew before anything is removed.
How do refunds work?
You have 7 days from purchase. Before activation, the refund is the full $499. After activation, it is $479 because a $20 domain & setup fee is non-refundable. You can start a request from the Billing tab.
