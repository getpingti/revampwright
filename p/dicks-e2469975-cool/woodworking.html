Custom Woodworking

Bars, tables, bookcases and mantels — built to look great for generations to come.

Proper construction techniques are vital for any woodworking project. Not only do these proper techniques help keep your kitchen, bath or closet project looking good. They also help our custom woodworking projects like bars, tables, bookcases and mantels look great for generations to come.

Dick's Custom Cabinets is so much more than a cabinet builder. Over the years we have had the honor to work on a wide range of custom projects. These projects are often work that they have a hard time finding a quality builder take the time to work on.

We can even finish these projects off with tile work, lighting and decorative stain, lacquer or paint finishes. Our finishing system allows us to custom mix our own stains, thus allowing us to match existing desired colors.
