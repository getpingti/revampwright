Counter Tops

Wood, laminate and Corian — matched to your cabinets.

Nothing quite accentuates the beauty of your cabinets like Corian counter tops. The molded sinks provide seamless bonding with the counter top, and Corian makes it so easy to keep it looking great for many years.

We also do Formica countertops. There is so much to choose from you’re sure to find the perfect match.

Counter tops are available in wood, laminate and Corian — matched to your cabinets and your kitchen.
