About Dick's Custom Cabinets

One major goal, since 1971

Founded in 1971. Dick's Custom Cabinets of Cornell, Illinois operates with one major goal: use customer ideas and our craftsmanship to create custom, one of a kind cabinetry, countertops and woodworking. Our mission is to provide our customers with the finest cabinetry at an affordable cost.

Take the step and contact us for a free consultation. We will use our detailed software to plan and create a visual layout that includes your design ideas along with functional design basics.

Upon approval our team of skilled craftsmen will begin creating your dream project using safety, patience and their skill and craftsmanship all the way through installation.

We take great pride in our creations. We use the finest hardwoods, operational hardware, decorative hardware, and finally a classy durable finish.

From kitchens to mantels

Our capabilities are not limited to cabinets. Kitchens, countertops, fireplace surrounds, entertainment centers, bathrooms, shower bases and surrounds — all custom, all built to order.

Community Involvement

Part of Cornell

Dick and Donna Leonard have always considered being involved in the community a high priority. As a member of the US Army, Dick Leonard served in Vietnam, volunteers on numerous church led work missions, and volunteers with numerous youth group organizations. Donna has devoted many volunteer hours towards local EMS systems, as well as involvement with their local church. Dick's Custom Cabinets has also hosted small workshops for young woodworkers involved in the 4-H programs, as well as donated cabinetry to the local 4-H fairgrounds.
