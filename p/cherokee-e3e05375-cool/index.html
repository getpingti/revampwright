Welcome

Welcome to Cherokee Cabinet

Born and raised in the Atlanta area, I have been designing and fabricating custom cabinetry for the metro area and surrounding counties since 1988. My father taught me the trade while working in his shop, then in 1988 I went out on my own to begin what has become a 25 year career of meeting people from all walks of life and making many long term customers and friends.

I enjoy creating kitchens, bathrooms, bookcases, and many other projects that people are proud to own. I have built my business one customer at a time and strictly by word of mouth referrals. I look forward to creating something for you
