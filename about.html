Who We Are

The English-language brand of Fangcun Design

RevampWright is the English-language brand of Fangcun Design, a website design studio based in Wuxi, Jiangsu, China. The studio is founder-led: experienced designers direct every project, and modern AI-assisted production handles the heavy lifting. That combination is how a rebuild that agencies price at $2,500–$4,000 can cost $499 here — the savings are structural, not a discount that expires.

Why small manufacturers and trade shops

Because we know the pattern: a great shop, a loyal customer base, and a website from 2012 that zooms sideways on a phone. These businesses don't need a marketing platform or a six-month branding process. They need their real work presented properly, at a price that doesn't require a committee.

How we work

  • Preview before payment. We build first. You judge the finished website, not a proposal.
  • Fixed price, in writing. $499 once, then $299/year for hosting and care. No hourly meters.
  • Straight about who we are. We're a China-based studio serving US small businesses online. No pretending to be local, no rented addresses — our support email is answered by the people who built your site.
  • Your website is yours. Connect your own domain anytime, update your contact details yourself, and if you ever leave, your site files can be exported on request.
