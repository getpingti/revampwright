How it works

Four steps, and the money only moves at step three.

1

We build your redesign

Our studio rebuilds your website from your current one — your products, photos and words, reorganized into a modern, mobile-first design.

2

You preview the finished site

You receive a private preview link by email. Browse the whole website on your phone and computer. Calls and contact details activate the moment the site is yours.

3

You buy it — if you love it

Pay the one-time $499 and enter your activation code. Your website goes live instantly on your own web address. Don't love it? Don't pay — the preview is deleted on the date promised.

4

Your domain & dashboard

Your free custom domain is connected within 24 hours. From your dashboard you can update your phone, email and address anytime, connect your old domain, and manage your care plan.

Common questions

Is my preview really the finished website?

Yes. Every page is complete and built from your real content. The only thing held back until activation is your live contact layer — phone numbers, email and the contact form — so the preview can't be mistaken for your working site.

What if I want changes?

Small fixes are part of the deal: your care plan includes two small content updates a year plus one free design style change a year (additional style changes are $100 each), and during your first days we'll correct anything we got wrong from your old site. You can also update your contact details and company intro yourself anytime in your dashboard. We don't take on new sections or major rebuilds — staying focused on redesigns is how we keep this fast and affordable.

Can I keep my current domain?

Yes. Your site goes live instantly on a free address we provide, and your dashboard walks you through pointing your existing domain to it whenever you're ready — free, self-service, with step-by-step instructions for your registrar.

What happens if I don't renew?

We'll remind you well ahead. If the care plan lapses, your site shows an owner login page for a 10-month grace period — log in anytime to renew and it's back instantly. After 10 months the site is taken offline.

Who is behind RevampWright?

RevampWright is an independent design studio working with small manufacturers and trade businesses across the US — everything is done online, and the people who answer your email are the people who built your site. See About.
